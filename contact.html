Before You Contact Us

A few useful answers.

Do I need to know exactly what software I need?

No. In many cases, selecting a tool before understanding the problem leads to poor results. Start by explaining the operational challenge.

Can SmartLink improve an existing system?

Yes. An engagement may involve improving an existing process or platform rather than replacing everything.

Can you work with organisations outside Port Harcourt?

Many systems, advisory and training engagements can be delivered remotely or through a hybrid approach depending on the project.

Do you provide ongoing support after implementation?

Ongoing support, optimisation and training can form part of the engagement depending on the organisation's requirements.
